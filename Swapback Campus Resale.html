How it works

Three steps, one walk across campus

Step 1

Snap and list

Take a few pics, pick a size and price. Your listing only shows to students nearby.

Buyers message you right in the app. No giving out your number to strangers.

Step 3

Meet and swap

Pick a public spot on campus, check the item in person, pay through the app.
